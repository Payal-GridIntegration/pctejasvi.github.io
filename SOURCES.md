## Research themes and design reference

The four research-theme groupings are editorial summaries derived from the titles and abstracts of the publications listed above: (1) multiphase AC–DC distribution systems and state estimation; (2) false data injection and smart-grid cyber-security; (3) EV aggregator bidding and electricity-market data integrity; and (4) optimization, machine learning and scientific computing.

The information architecture is inspired by the Sustainability Lab reference site: https://sustainability-lab.github.io/. The colour palette, typography, illustrations, diagrams, layout and written copy are original.
