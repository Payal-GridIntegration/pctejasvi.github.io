ABOUT

Curiosity grounded
in real systems.

PB.

My research focuses on the technical challenges that emerge as electricity networks become more distributed, interconnected and digitally controlled.

At IIT Gandhinagar, I pursued doctoral research in Electrical Engineering, with a thesis on the vulnerability of smart grids to false data injection attacks. This work brought together distribution-system modelling, state estimation and cyber-security — questions that remain central to reliable grid operation.

My publication record also explores multiphase AC–DC power-flow algorithms, converter-aware state estimation, electric-vehicle aggregator bidding and learning-based approaches to data manipulation. More recently, my interests have extended to scientific software and developer tools through MinTEJ, a terminal-based editor for Julia, and pAST.jl, a Julia program for abstract syntax tree visualization and description.
