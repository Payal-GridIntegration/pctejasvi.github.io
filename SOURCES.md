## Research themes represented

The four research-theme groupings on the site are editorial summaries derived from the titles and abstracts of the publications above: (1) multiphase AC–DC distribution systems and state estimation; (2) false data injection and smart-grid cyber-security; (3) EV aggregator bidding and electricity-market data integrity; and (4) optimization, machine learning and scientific computing.
